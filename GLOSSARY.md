# DecisionDrift

DecisionDrift helps employees recognize when their current work may conflict with an approved company decision.

## Language

**Candidate decision**:
A statement extracted from source material that may represent a company agreement but has not been confirmed by a designated person.
_Avoid_: Approved decision, policy

**Approved decision**:
A company agreement confirmed by a designated person, with a source, scope, and any stated date constraints.
_Avoid_: Suggestion, candidate decision

**Decision source**:
The original material supporting a decision, identified by its document identity and relevant passage.

**Decision scope**:
The circumstances in which an approved decision applies.

**Possible conflict**:
An evidence-backed indication that an employee's observed work may contradict an applicable approved decision. It is not a determination that the employee has violated a rule.
_Avoid_: Violation, compliance failure
